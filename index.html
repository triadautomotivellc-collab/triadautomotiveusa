Straight answers

Before you ask.

The sourcing program is meant to make long-distance buying more informed—not pretend risk disappears.

Do you guarantee a South Texas vehicle is rust-free?

No. South Texas vehicles generally avoid years of road-salt exposure, but every vehicle is different. We treat rust-free / low-corrosion as the target and verify visible condition as far as the available inspection and media allow.

Can you find a specific truck, SUV, classic, or project?

That is the point of the request. Send the exact platform, acceptable years, drivetrain, mileage, budget, color preferences and deal-breakers. The tighter the target, the smarter the search.

Can TRIAD source parts from Mexico?

Yes, for parts that are lawful to source/import, available, and compatible with your application. Availability, shipping, customs, fitment and warranty can vary, so those details are handled case by case.

Do I have to buy the first vehicle you find?

No. A candidate should fit the request and make financial sense. The process is designed to give you information before you make a purchase decision.

Can you help get the vehicle to Wisconsin?

TRIAD can help coordinate transport and logistics when a candidate is approved. Exact options and pricing depend on location, vehicle condition, timing, and carrier availability.
